The brief

A home services company offering 24-hour emergency call-outs needed to be found by people searching in a panic, and needed a way to track the leads that came in.

What I did

  • Four-page website with all Italian copy: home, services, about, contact.
  • Google Business Profile set up and filled in.
  • A lead tracker so enquiries stop getting lost.
  • A plan for Meta and Google Maps advertising.

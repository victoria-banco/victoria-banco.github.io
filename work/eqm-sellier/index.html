EQM Sellier

Sector
Artisan saddlery
Location
Adro, Franciacorta
Year
2026
Built with
Custom Shopify theme
Status
Built, launch pending

The brief

A workshop making bespoke saddles, bridles and competition trunks by hand needed to move from an old WordPress site to a real shop, without losing the consultation-first way it sells a saddle that costs several thousand euros.

What I did

  • A complete custom Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme, in Italian and English.
  • A bespoke saddle page with a visual timeline, leather swatches and a quote form tracked as a funnel.
  • A repair request form for the workshop.
  • Cart drawer, predictive search, filters and a sticky buy button on mobile.
  • Product pages ready for 3D and augmented reality using Shopify's native model viewer.
  • Structured data for products, organisation, breadcrumbs, articles and FAQ.
  • A WordPress to Shopify redirect map, a keyword map and a Journal plan.
  • Email recovery flows, analytics setup and a plain-language handover document.

Details worth noticing

  • The consultation-first flow for high-ticket bespoke products: the aim is a conversation, not a checkout.
  • Augmented reality loads only on products that have a model, so the rest of the shop stays fast.
  • A palette and type system built into theme settings, so the client can change the look without touching code.

Result

Ready to launch, waiting on the client's photography and final prices.

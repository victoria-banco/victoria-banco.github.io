The brief

A houseboat holiday rental needed a site that turns visitors into booking enquiries, for guests arriving from across Europe.

What I did

  • First version built on Wix, handed over with a 29-page guide in February 2026.
  • Rebuilt in October 2026 as a fast hand-coded site connected to bookings.
  • Copy, structure, design, build and hosting setup.

Result

The owner compared both versions and chose the new one. It is moving to his own domain and his own Netlify account.

Questions

What does a brand strategist actually do?

A brand strategist decides what a business should stand for, who it speaks to and how it says it, before any design or advertising begins. In practice that means positioning, a messaging structure, and a tone of voice. Design makes a brand look consistent. Strategy makes it mean something consistent.

What is the difference between translation and cultural adaptation?

Translation carries words across languages. Adaptation carries meaning across markets. The same claim does different work in different places: quality reads as engineering and reliability in German, and closer to care and craftsmanship in Italian. A translated tagline can be correct and still land flat. Adaptation rewrites for the effect the original was designed to have, which usually means changing the words.

What does SEO have to do with brand strategy?

Search is where positioning gets tested. Keyword research shows the words your market actually uses for its problem, and those are often not the words a business uses for its solution. Closing that gap improves rankings and sharpens the brand at the same time.

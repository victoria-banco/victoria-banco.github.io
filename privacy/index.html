Who runs this site

Victoria Clara Swart, independent professional, Rome, Italy. P.IVA 18420501001. Email: victoria.c.swart@icloud.com.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. It uses no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What the contact form collects

When you send the form, your name, email address and message are processed by Formspree and forwarded to me by email. The form also records which language version of the site you used, and, if you arrived from a link on a client's website, which site sent you. That data is used only to answer you. It is not sold and not shared for marketing.

Language preference

If you are offered a different language version, that suggestion comes from your browser's own language setting. No location lookup is done on your IP address and nothing is sent to a third party. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and never leaves your device.

Fonts

Fonts are served from this site's own server. No request is made to Google Fonts or any other font service.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU or EEA you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data you have sent me. Write to victoria.c.swart@icloud.com and I will answer within the period the law allows.

How long it is kept

Form messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the work that followed.

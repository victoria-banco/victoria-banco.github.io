What does a brand strategist actually do?

A brand strategist decides what a business should stand for, who it speaks to, and how it says it — before any design or advertising begins. In practice this means positioning (what makes you distinct and defensible), messaging architecture (your core message and how it adapts by audience and channel), and brand voice (the vocabulary and tone that make you recognisable). Design makes a brand look consistent; strategy makes it mean something consistent.

What is the difference between translation and cultural adaptation?

Translation carries words across languages. Cultural adaptation carries meaning across markets. The same claim does different work in different places: "quality" reads as engineering and reliability in German, and closer to care and craftsmanship in Italian. A translated tagline can be technically correct and still land flat, or signal the wrong price tier entirely. Adaptation rewrites for the effect the original was designed to have — which usually means changing the words.

What does SEO have to do with brand strategy?

Search is where positioning gets tested. Keyword research reveals the language your market actually uses to describe its problem, which frequently differs from the language a business uses to describe its solution. Closing that gap improves rankings and sharpens the brand at the same time. Increasingly it also determines whether AI answer engines can describe your business correctly when someone asks about your category.
